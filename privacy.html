1. Scope and identity

Mourish AI Lite is a chat service published at mourish-ai-lite.vercel.app by Mourish AI Lite ("we", "us"). This policy covers the website, the chat interface, and the optional integrations you choose to connect.

It does not cover third-party websites you reach through a link, or services you connect that have their own privacy terms. For all privacy questions, the single contact address is nexasblockify@gmail.com.

3. Information we never hold

  • Your encryption PIN.
  • The key derived from your PIN.
  • The readable text of your encrypted messages.
  • Your decrypted conversation history.

These exist only in your browser while the tab is open, or in your own exports.

4. How encryption works

  1. Your PIN is combined with a random salt and stretched through PBKDF2 using SHA-256 over 100,000 iterations, producing a 256-bit key.
  2. Each message is sealed with AES-256-GCM and a fresh random IV. GCM authenticates the ciphertext, so any edit is detectable.
  3. A SHA-256 digest of the message is stored so you can later confirm nothing changed.
  4. Only the ciphertext, IV, salt, and digest are transmitted.
Precision matters SHA-256 is a fingerprint, not a lock. It proves integrity. Confidentiality comes from AES-256-GCM. We describe both accurately so you can judge the guarantee for yourself.

5. Access only when you ask

If you connect a workspace account, the assistant does not browse it. Data is read or acted on only when a message explicitly requests that action — for example, "summarise the file I shared" or "create a calendar event for Thursday at 3pm".

  • No background syncing of mail, files, calendars, or documents.
  • No scanning of your account to build a profile.
  • No access beyond the specific item or action your message names.
  • You can disconnect at any time, which stops all future access.
Important boundary Encryption protects what we store. When you ask the assistant to act on a connected account, the relevant content must be processed in readable form for that request to work. This happens only on your explicit instruction and only for the duration of that request.

6. How we use information

  • To run the chat, route your message to the agent you selected, and return a reply.
  • To carry out the workspace actions you request.
  • To keep the service available and safe: abuse prevention, rate limiting, fault diagnosis.
  • To answer you when you email us.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your content to train models, to target advertising, or to build behavioural profiles.

7. Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a stated basis, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the service you asked for.
  • Consent — for connecting third-party accounts, which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — security, abuse prevention, and service reliability, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose something by law.

8. Cookies and local storage

We keep this minimal.

  • Essential cookies — session and sign-in state. Required for the service to function.
  • Local storage — interface preferences such as the last agent you selected.
  • No advertising cookies and no third-party tracking pixels.

Clearing site data removes locally stored preferences. Encrypted history stored under your account is unaffected and remains until you delete it.

9. Connected third-party services

Integrations are optional. When you connect one, you authorise a specific set of permissions, and you can revoke them from your provider's own security settings at any time. Names of third-party products appear on this site only to describe compatibility and remain the trademarks of their owners.

Our use of data received from connected services is limited to performing the action you requested. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or allow staff to read it except where you ask us to act on it, or where needed for security or legal compliance.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Encrypted chats — kept until you delete them or close your account.
  • Technical records — up to 30 days.
  • Connection tokens — until you disconnect or delete your account, then removed within 30 days.
  • Support email — up to 24 months, so we can track recurring issues.

Deleting a chat removes its stored ciphertext. Because we never held the plaintext or your key, there is nothing else to erase.

11. Sharing and processors

We share data only with providers needed to operate the service, under written data-processing terms:

  • Hosting and content delivery providers.
  • AI model providers, to generate replies for the request in progress. Under our terms they do not retain content for training.
  • Identity and integration providers, only when you choose to connect them.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

12. International transfers

Our providers may process data in other countries. Where required, transfers rely on recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, and we take reasonable steps to ensure equivalent protection.

13. Security measures

  • Encryption in transit for all traffic.
  • Encryption at rest for connection tokens and account records.
  • Strict access controls and least-privilege permissions for staff.
  • Logging and alerting for unusual access patterns.
  • Client-side encryption for message content, so a storage breach does not expose readable conversations.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and any regulator as required by law.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your data, and to object to certain processing or withdraw consent.

To exercise any right, email nexasblockify@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify account ownership. There is no charge for reasonable requests.

One honest caveat: we cannot show you the contents of your encrypted messages, because we cannot read them. You can decrypt them yourself in the app at any time, and export them if you wish.

15. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age set by local law. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the service, contact us and we will remove the account.

16. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you through automated processing. AI replies are generated for your own use and are not used to score, rank, or decide anything about you.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app and the review date above will change. Continuing to use the service after an update means you accept the revised policy.

18. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, contact nexasblockify@gmail.com first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

19. Contact

All privacy enquiries, requests, and complaints: nexasblockify@gmail.com. We aim to reply within two business days and to resolve formal requests within 30 days.
