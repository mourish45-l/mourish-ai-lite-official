5. Encryption and your PIN

Message content is encrypted in your browser using a key derived from your PIN through PBKDF2 with SHA-256, then sealed with AES-256-GCM and fingerprinted with a SHA-256 digest.

No recovery, by design We do not store your PIN or your derived key. If you forget your PIN, your encrypted history cannot be decrypted by you, by us, or by any administrator. There is no reset process. Store your PIN safely before relying on this for anything you may need later.
What hashing does and does not do SHA-256 is a one-way fingerprint used to detect change. It does not hide content by itself. Confidentiality comes from AES-256-GCM. Do not treat a hash as encryption.
