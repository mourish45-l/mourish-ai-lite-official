Overview

A small tool with one opinion: your chats are yours

Most chatbots ask you to trust a policy page. Mourish AI Lite removes the need for that trust by making the encryption happen on your device. We built it for people who want a capable assistant without handing over a readable transcript of their work, health notes, drafts, or code.

Honest about limits

How it works

Three steps, no setup

Pick an agent

Choose the role that fits the task. You can change it between messages without losing context.

Write and set a PIN

Type your message and choose a PIN. The PIN stays in your browser and is never transmitted or stored.

Encrypt, then send

Your browser derives a key, encrypts the message, adds a SHA-256 fingerprint, and sends only the sealed result.

Security model

Designed so administrators can't read your chats

Encryption happens in your browser. The server receives sealed data, not readable text.

✓

PBKDF2 key derivation

Your PIN is stretched with 100,000 SHA-256 iterations and a random salt to build a 256-bit key.

✓

AES-256-GCM encryption

Each message uses a fresh random IV. GCM also authenticates the ciphertext, so edits are detected.

✓

SHA-256 integrity hash

A one-way fingerprint is stored per message so you can verify content was not altered.

✓

No plaintext on the server

We receive ciphertext, IV, salt, and hash. There is no readable copy to leak.

✓

No admin key

Staff can manage accounts but hold no decryption key. There is no backdoor to build or abuse.

✓

No silent data access

Connected workspace data is read only when you ask for it in a message — never on a schedule.

Questions

Straight answers

Can Mourish AI or an administrator read my messages?

No. The key is derived from your PIN inside your browser and never transmitted. What reaches the server is encrypted data plus a one-way hash. Without your PIN there is nothing readable to open.

Does the assistant read my connected accounts on its own?

No. Workspace data is only read or acted on when you explicitly ask for it in a message, such as "summarise the document I shared". Nothing is fetched in the background.

Is SHA-256 the same as encryption?

No, and we do not pretend otherwise. SHA-256 is a one-way fingerprint used to detect changes. Confidentiality comes from AES-256-GCM. We use both, for different jobs.

What happens if I forget my PIN?

Your encrypted history becomes unreadable. We cannot reset it because we never held it. Keep a copy of your PIN in a password manager.

Do you train models on my messages?

No. Content is not used to train models and is not sold or used for advertising.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the demo and basic chat without signing in. An account is only needed if you want to keep encrypted history or connect a workspace account.
